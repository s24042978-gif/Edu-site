Admissions guide

Which degree are you planning to pursue?

Entry requirements depend on the university, course, teaching language, and intake year.

01 / UNDERGRADUATE

Bachelor's degree

Usually requires completed secondary education equivalent to 12 years of schooling.

  • SSC and HSC certificates/transcripts, or equivalent
  • Proof of English or Chinese proficiency, if required
  • CSCA or other entrance assessment, if required
  • Personal statement and additional documents as requested
02 / POSTGRADUATE

Master's degree

Usually requires a recognized bachelor's degree relevant to the intended program.

  • Bachelor's certificate and complete transcripts
  • CV and study plan/personal statement
  • Recommendation letters, where required
  • Language evidence and interview, if required
03 / RESEARCH

PhD / Doctoral degree

Usually requires a relevant master's degree, although specific pathways may differ.

  • Degree certificates and transcripts
  • Academic CV and research proposal
  • Recommendation letters
  • Supervisor contact or interview, where required
Typical duration only: many bachelor's programs take around 4 years, master's programs around 2–3 years, and PhD programs around 3–4 years or longer. Medicine, architecture, and other specialist programs can differ.
